About StrokeHealth

StrokeHealth demonstrates how machine learning can be applied to healthcare-related data to explore patterns associated with stroke risk.

🧠

AI Prediction

A trained machine-learning pipeline processes selected demographic and health-related features to estimate stroke-risk probability.

📊

Risk Visualization

Prediction results are presented through an interactive interface designed to make model output easier to understand.

Research Direction

StrokeHealth is positioned as an ongoing research and experimentation project rather than a clinical diagnostic system.

The project explores the intersection of machine learning, biomedical engineering and healthcare data.

Future research directions may include improved model generalization, interpretability, data quality analysis, and evaluation on broader datasets.

Research & Educational Prototype

StrokeHealth is intended for research and educational purposes only. It is not a medical diagnostic device and should not be used to make medical decisions or replace professional medical advice.
